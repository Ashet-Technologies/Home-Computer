# User Expansion design review — 2026-10-24

**Verdict: NOT READY for fabrication release.** Connectivity and the basic perfboard concept match the intended card, but configured PCB rules still fail and several prototyping details should be corrected before ordering.

Automated review coverage is complete: 2 schematic files, 3 sheet instances, 151 symbol instances, 874 PCB footprints, and 957 pads. No unresolved symbols, failed pad queries, or aggregate audit diagnostics were reported. Physical enclosure fit, fabrication outputs, and the actual manufacturer's order preview remain unverified; this report does not approve a fabrication order.

Reviewed the saved `PCB/User Expansion.kicad_sch` hierarchy and the exact open `PCB/User Expansion.kicad_pcb`. Used Konnect 0.13.0 and KiCad 10.0.6. The board was saved through IPC before checks. No circuit, routing, footprint, rule, or source-file fixes were applied. The second DRC used analysis-only zone refill without saving that refill to the live board. No Zig command was needed.

The schematic evidence covers the saved files. The tool could not bind the schematic editor's live document state, so unsaved schematic editor changes are outside this review.

## Critical — resolve or deliberately justify before release (3 groups)

These are configured-rule release blockers, not evidence that all the geometry is beyond every manufacturer's capabilities. There are no reported copper shorts, and the only unrouted items involve the accepted B1 omission.

1. **U2's footprint violates the active clearance rule: 6 errors.** Adjacent VSSOP pads have 0.150 mm clearance against the Default netclass's 0.200 mm requirement. Both offending pads belong to U2; moving the component cannot change their spacing. Use a verified package land pattern and a narrowly scoped clearance rule compatible with the selected fabricator, or revise the land pattern while preserving solderability. Do not globally relax clearance just to clear the report. The global minimum reported by `get_design_rules` is 0.1651 mm; the active netclass explains the stricter 0.200 mm DRC requirement.

2. **Via annular rings violate the configured minimum: 23 errors.** These vias have 0.150 mm rings against a 0.200 mm rule. Examples include GND at (198.882, 92.710) and identity supply at (197.485, 104.648). Increase the via diameter by 0.100 mm at the existing drill size to obtain 0.200 mm rings, subject to clearance rechecking; alternatively, obtain explicit fabrication acceptance for 0.150 mm rings and set the corresponding rule deliberately. Use Konnect routing/verification tools to replace the affected vias or establish the accepted rule, followed by DRC.

3. **Thermal connections do not meet the two-spoke rule: 16 non-waived errors.** DRC reports 17 one-spoke thermal violations, including one on the waived B1 contact. Other affected locations include TP1329, TP133, TP136, configurable-row tap TP135, and connector contacts including B10 `/PRESENT`. These are not additional reported open circuits, but the copper connections fail the chosen rule. Adjust the relevant zone/thermal geometry, use intentional solid connections where appropriate, or justify a local one-spoke rule from the actual connection geometry and current. Refill and rerun DRC. The zone geometry belongs to the board; the pad locations belong to their named footprints.

## Warnings — fix or document (6 groups)

1. **Neither IC has local decoupling.** The exported hierarchy and PCB inventory contain no capacitors. Add 100 nF near U1 VCC–GND and near each U2 supply, keeping the identity and switched supply domains separate. TI recommends approximately 100 nF close to both U2 supply pins. Use Konnect schematic component/wiring tools, synchronize the PCB carefully, place and route the capacitors, then repeat ERC/DRC. [TI TCA9517A datasheet, sections 9–10](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tca9517a.pdf).

2. **The 0.700 mm holes restrict useful prototyping parts.** All 867 through-hole pads use 1.500 mm copper and 0.700 mm drills. Common 0.1-inch headers use approximately 0.635 mm square posts, whose diagonal is about 0.898 mm, so they will not fit these holes. If ordinary headers and sockets are intended, use an appropriate finished-hole specification, commonly around 1.0 mm after checking the actual part. A 1.500/1.000 mm pad/hole leaves a nominal 0.250 mm annular ring. Small wire and narrow component leads can still suit the present holes. Change the footprint/pad definition through Konnect and recheck pours and clearances. [Example: Samtec TSW square-post header](https://suddendocs.samtec.com/catalog_english/tsw_th.pdf).

3. **The I²S breakout does not share the main 0.1-inch grid.** TP123–TP127 are at x=194.310 mm; the main signal column is x=187.960 mm. The 6.350 mm separation is 2.5 pitches, so the audio column is offset by 1.270 mm from the common grid. Its own vertical spacing is correctly 2.540 mm. Move the five audio pads by 1.270 mm horizontally to a common-grid column if the requirement is one shared grid. TP137 also has a harmless 0.006 mm placement typo (y=134.626 instead of 134.620); snap it during cleanup. Use Konnect footprint movement and reroute only the affected connections.

4. **Define a usable default for the I²C and EEPROM jumpers.** JP101 center correctly connects to U1 WP, and JP102 center correctly connects to U2 VCCA. Both footprints are open jumpers. Leaving JP101 open leaves WP undefined; leaving JP102 open leaves U2's A-side reference supply unpowered. Specify the assembled bridge positions or provide suitable default bias/bridges. JP101 2–3 selects write enabled; 1–2 selects write protected. JP102 2–3 selects main 3.3 V; 1–2 selects main 5 V; never bridge both supply selections. The user-side SCL/SDA nets have no pull-ups: add footprints tied to the selected user-side supply, or clearly require the user's circuit to supply them. The identity-side 10 kΩ pull-ups must stay on `+3V3_ID`. Each side of the repeater needs pull-ups; Microchip requires WP tied to VCC or GND. [TI datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tca9517a.pdf), [Microchip 24LC256 datasheet, section 2.4](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/MPD/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/24AA256-24LC256-24FC256-256K-I2C-Serial-EEPROM-DS20001203.pdf).

5. **Clean up schematic/ERC and parity bookkeeping.** ERC reports 24 errors and 43 warnings: 22 pin-type conflicts, one undriven U2 VCCA power pin through the open selector, one orphan GND symbol `#PWR0142`, 41 off-grid warnings, and two library-symbol mismatches. The connector's multiple power-output pin declarations explain the conflicts; these do not prove multiple real supplies are shorted. Correct the connector symbol's electrical model and source marking, remove the orphan symbol, and review the two modified jumper symbols. Independent full netlist-to-pad comparison found exactly one missing schematic footprint, TP134 (GND), and zero net-assignment mismatches among corresponding pads. Restore TP134 or remove its obsolete schematic symbol. Preserve the 768 intentional PCB-only perf pads when synchronizing; do not blindly delete extra footprints. A synchronization dry run was refused because the schematic editor was open, and made no changes.

6. **Improve the fabrication silkscreen and drawing consistency.** The saved-file DRC has 166 silk-overlap warnings, 34 text-height warnings, one silk-over-copper warning, one altered JP101 library footprint warning, and one dangling-track warning. Analysis-only refill changes the silk warnings slightly but leaves all error counts unchanged. Many overlaps are connected-row markings crossing test-pad footprint circles. Remove redundant circles/markings and make functional labels readable. Inspect JP101's intentional local changes before updating it from the library. Remove the dangling track if it is unnecessary. DRC ownership identifies which artwork belongs to the footprint and which belongs to the board.

## Fabrication and bench acceptance still needed

- Specify the actual manufacturer's accepted clearance, via annular ring, hole tolerances, card thickness, finger finish, and bevel. Confirm the connector's mechanical mating requirements and enclosure fit. The automated manufacturing validator used JLCPCB from configuration/defaults; the user did not select that vendor for this review.
- After corrections, generate and inspect Gerbers and plated-drill outputs, including pad isolation, the connected rows, connector key/edge, and copper/mask around fingers. Check the real order preview. No fabrication package was created or approved in this review.
- Assemble/program U1, set JP101/JP102, and verify EEPROM reads at 0x57 while main rails are off. Check that the identity domain does not back-power the user rails. Verify the user-side I²C pull-ups and rise times at the intended speed. The fitted 10 kΩ identity pull-ups are not by themselves a guarantee of 400 kHz operation under arbitrary capacitive loading.
- Treat GP/HSTX/I²S breakouts as experimental interfaces. Full-speed signal integrity and a populated user's circuit were not qualified by ERC/DRC.
