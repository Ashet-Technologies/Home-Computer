# User Expansion review — 2026-10-25

**Verdict: NEEDS ATTENTION for the shared-grid requirement; no remaining PCB fabrication blocker found in the saved design.** Both DRC runs report **zero errors and zero unrouted connections**. The previous clearance, annular-ring, thermal-spoke, and missing-footprint issues are resolved. The I²S breakout still does not share the main 0.1-inch grid. Assembly requires deliberate jumper settings and user-side I²C pull-ups.

This review uses the intended scope: a manually assembled experimentation perfboard. A1/B1 isolation, omitted SLOT breakouts, and omitted local decoupling are accepted decisions. The decoupling waiver is recorded below and does not block this verdict.

## Remaining findings

1. **Warning — I²S pads remain half a grid step away from the common grid.** TP123–TP127 are at x=194.310 mm, while the main breakout column is x=187.960 mm. Their 6.350 mm separation is 2.5 pitches of 2.540 mm. The audio pads have correct vertical pitch, but a rigid 0.1-inch assembly cannot span both columns on one common grid. Move the audio column horizontally by 1.270 mm if one shared grid remains the requirement. This is a prototyping usability issue, not a fabrication clearance error. TP137 also retains the harmless 0.006 mm y-position typo (134.626 instead of 134.620 mm).

2. **Warning — define the assembly state of JP101/JP102 and the user-side I²C bus.** The exported connections are correct, but both jumpers are open in the manufactured copper. JP101 center goes to U1 WP: bridge 2–3 for writes, or 1–2 for write protection; do not leave WP floating. JP102 center goes to U2 VCCA: bridge 2–3 for main 3.3 V, or 1–2 for main 5 V; never bridge both selections. TP132 SCL and TP142 SDA require pull-ups in the user's circuit to the chosen A-side supply. The identity-side 10 kΩ pull-ups are present on the separate identity supply. These are assembly/use requirements for this perfboard, rather than a demand to add more circuitry. See the [TI TCA9517A datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tca9517a.pdf) and [Microchip 24LC256 datasheet](https://ww1.microchip.com/downloads/aemDocuments/documents/MPD/ProductDocuments/DataSheets/24AA256-24LC256-24FC256-256K-I2C-Serial-EEPROM-DS20001203.pdf).

3. **Warning — ERC and library bookkeeping still need cleanup.** ERC reports 21 errors and 43 warnings. Twenty errors are connector pin-type conflicts on intentionally common supply/GND contacts, including the required PRESENT-to-GND connection; direct connectivity does not show distinct supplies shorted. The remaining error is U2 VCCA not driven through the open JP102 selector. Warnings comprise 39 schematic off-grid endpoints, two jumper-symbol/library mismatches, and two missing-library references for H102/H103 (`Mechanical Prototype`). Resolve the connector's electrical pin model, document the assembly selector, and register or relink the bracket symbols. The embedded symbols and their PCB footprints were resolved in the exported design, so these library warnings did not prevent connectivity review. The old orphan power symbol and obsolete TP134 omission are resolved.

4. **Suggestion — clean up silkscreen and a tiny trace stub.** The 408 DRC warnings comprise 199 extra-footprint messages, 106 silk overlaps, 66 silk-over-copper messages, 34 undersized-text messages, two silk-to-edge messages, and one dangling track. Extra footprints are intentional bare perf pads; a complete independent inventory establishes 756 such pads, rather than relying on KiCad's shorter warning list. The two edge warnings are H102/H103's own outline rectangles touching the board edge, not electrical clearances. Functional labels should remain readable after the fabricator clips silk against mask openings. The dangling B.Cu segment is only 0.0028 mm long on +3.3VP at (198.628, 104.648); remove it during cleanup. It does not represent an unrouted net. Footprint-owned artwork should be corrected in the footprint rather than by moving otherwise correctly placed parts.

## Accepted exceptions

- **No local decoupling:** explicitly accepted by the user for this experimentation prototype. The aggregate audit's three IC-power-pin findings are retained as a noise/transient reliability risk, not fabrication blockers. Its additional generic demand for capacitors on every exposed rail does not establish a separate defect in an unpopulated perfboard.
- **A1/B1 disconnected:** redundant GND contacts deliberately isolated and marked NC. Accepted.
- **SLOT signals omitted:** accepted static configuration signals, outside the required hand-wired breakout scope.
- **Uncommitted RESET and COM:** final circuitry and any RESET bias belong to the user's experiment. No generic reset RC network is required for the bare board.
- **Manual assembly:** fiducials, automated-assembly part sourcing, and production-line BOM completeness are outside scope. Bare test pads are copper features, not missing procurement items.
